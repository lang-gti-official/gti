Add signed chrono microseconds, milliseconds, seconds, minutes and hours through a shared `duration<Unit>` family. `try_cast<To>()` reports overflow through `expected`; `cast<To>()` uses the same checked conversion and panics on overflow. Both truncate toward zero and support constexpr evaluation. Add same-unit comparisons, checked addition/subtraction and duration formatting. Existing nanosecond clock APIs remain available.
