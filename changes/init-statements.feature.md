`if`, `if constexpr`, `switch` and range `for` accept a C++17/C++20 init statement, as in `if (auto& name = names[index]; !name.empty())`, `switch (Shape shape = make_shape(); shape)` and `for (auto items = load(); auto& item : items)`. The init's names are scoped to the whole statement, every branch and `else` included, and its objects are destroyed exactly once when the statement ends, including on `break`, `continue`, `return` and `?` exits. An `if constexpr` init must be a `constexpr` declaration. The formatter, Tree-sitter grammar and language server keep the written header form.
