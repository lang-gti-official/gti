title: <std/cmath>

kind: library-header
api:
  - std/cmath|function|std::abs
  - std/cmath|function|std::isfinite
  - std/cmath|function|std::isinf
  - std/cmath|function|std::isnan
examples: []
---

```gti
#include <std/cmath>
```

The current executable surface is deliberately restricted to `float`.
`abs` preserves ordinary floating-point behavior, while the classification
functions inspect the binary32 exponent and significand. Other familiar
`<cmath>` declarations remain bodyless and must not be called yet.
