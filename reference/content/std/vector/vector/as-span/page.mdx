---
title: std::vector::as_span
description: Returns a checked read-only span over the vector's live elements.
kind: member
api:
  - std/vector|method|std::vector::as_span
examples: []
---

The result covers the vector's logical size, not its reserved capacity. It
allocates nothing and retains a read-only borrow of the vector storage, so
movement, reallocation, and mutation remain unavailable while the span is
live.
