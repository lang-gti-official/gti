---
title: std::vector::back
description: Borrows the final element through a read-only or mutable receiver.
kind: member
since: 0.120.0
api:
  - std/vector|method|std::vector::back
examples:
  - read-and-update-back
---

Returns a borrow of the last element. A read-only receiver produces `T&`; a
mutable receiver can produce `mut T&` for in-place modification.

The vector must not be empty. Empty access follows GTI's defined bounds-failure
path. The returned borrow remains tied to the vector for its lifetime.
